Your panels

Photograph your own panels over a few days. Solara reads the dust in each photograph, works out the power it is costing you, and plans your next wash against the real rain forecast for your location.

Your photographs

  • At least 4 photographs, at most 40
  • From at least 3 different days, so Solara can see the dust building up
  • JPG, PNG or WebP, at least 192 pixels on the short side, up to 20 MB each
  • Same panel, same spot, around midday; keep the panel in the middle of the frame
