How the test works

Real weather

Daily sunshine, rain and airborne dust from NASA POWER for each park, 2005 to 2024. Every policy is tuned on 2005 to 2019 and judged on 2020 to 2024.

Indian soiling

On dry days dust costs about 0.4% of output a day, the rate measured in Delhi, Gandhinagar and north India, rising with the dust in the air. Rain of 5 mm or more washes most of it off.

Rupees and litres

Lost energy is worth ₹2.60 per kWh. One wash of a megawatt costs about ₹9,500 in labour and 15,000 litres of water.

Modules 1 and 2 in the loop

The scheduler never sees the true dust. It sees Module 2's daily reading, with Module 2's real measured error, re-zeroed after each wash.
