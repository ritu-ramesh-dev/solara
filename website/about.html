Dust is a desert problem

India's biggest solar parks sit where the sun is strongest, and that is where the dust is too. Reported losses from soiling range from a few percent to more than 40% of a panel's output.

Cleaning on a fixed calendar wastes water and labour on glass that is still clean, then lets losses build up between visits. Solara cleans when the panel itself says so.

The project

One pipeline: see the dust, price it, decide when to wash.

The panel on the home page

The 3D module is drawn to the datasheet of the 540 to 550 W class now installed at Indian utility parks: 144 half-cut monocrystalline PERC cells on 182 mm wafers, ten busbars, 2279 by 1134 by 35 mm, a C-profile aluminium frame, three split junction boxes and MC4 connectors. The dust on its glass is Module 3's simulated soiling at Bhadla Solar Park, and the light in its reflections is a real desert dusk sky.
